# Sunrise Dental Clinic - Patient FAQ

## Clinic timings
We are open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 7 pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
Emergency consultations are available on Sundays between 10 am and 1 pm by phone only.

## Location and parking
Sunrise Dental Clinic, 12 MG Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038. We are a 5 minute walk from the
Indiranagar metro station. Free parking is available behind the building.

## Consultation fees
A general dental consultation costs Rs 500 and includes an oral examination and a treatment plan.
The consultation fee is waived if you go ahead with any treatment on the same day.

## Teeth cleaning
Professional cleaning (scaling and polishing) costs Rs 1,500 and takes about 45 minutes.
We recommend a cleaning every six months.

## Teeth whitening
Professional teeth whitening costs Rs 8,000 per session and takes about 60 minutes.
Results typically last 12 to 18 months with good oral hygiene. Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy.

## Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment starts at Rs 6,500 per tooth, plus Rs 7,000 for a ceramic crown if required.
Most root canals are completed in one or two visits under local anaesthesia.

## Braces and aligners
Metal braces start at Rs 35,000 and clear aligners start at Rs 90,000 for a full course.
A braces consultation with our orthodontist, Dr. Kavya Rao, is free.

## Dental implants
Single tooth implants start at Rs 30,000 including the crown. A CBCT scan (Rs 2,500) is required before planning.

## Payment options
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards and cash. No-cost EMI is available on treatments above Rs 20,000
through Bajaj Finserv.

## Insurance
We accept cashless insurance from Star Health, HDFC Ergo and ICICI Lombard for eligible procedures.
For other insurers we provide all documents for reimbursement.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment. You can do this in the chat
by sharing your booking reference and phone number.
