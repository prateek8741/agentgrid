## Home loans
We work with SBI, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank for home loans at 8.4 to 9.1 percent interest.
Most buyers can get up to 80 percent of the property value as a loan. Our team helps with the paperwork for free.

## Site visits
Site visits are available every day from 10 am to 5 pm, including weekends. A relationship manager will
accompany you, and we can arrange free pick-up within 10 km of the project.

## Booking amount and payment plan
The booking amount is Rs 2 lakh for apartments and Rs 5 lakh for villas. Under-construction projects follow
a construction-linked payment plan.

## Documents required
PAN card, Aadhaar card, last 3 months salary slips or ITR for 2 years, and 6 months of bank statements.
